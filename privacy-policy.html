Privacy Policy

ABC Kids A to Z

Effective date: September 18, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how ABC Kids A to Z handles information when you use the app.

Simple summary: ABC Kids A to Z is designed as an offline children's learning app. The current version does not require an account, does not contain advertising, and does not intentionally collect or share personal information.

1. Information We Collect

The current version of ABC Kids A to Z does not intentionally collect, request, or store personal information such as a child's name, email address, phone number, precise location, photographs, contacts, or account credentials.

The app does not require users to create an account or sign in.

2. How the App Works

The learning activities, letter content, games, and visual content are provided locally within the app. The app does not require an internet connection for its core learning features.

The app may use the Android device's built-in text-to-speech functionality to pronounce learning words when the user chooses the speech feature. The app itself does not create or maintain a speech-data profile.

3. Information Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or intentionally share personal information with advertisers, data brokers, or other third parties.

The current app does not include advertising, analytics SDKs, social-login services, or third-party account systems.

4. Children's Privacy

ABC Kids A to Z is designed for children and families. We take children's privacy seriously and do not intentionally collect personal information from children through the app.

Parents and guardians should contact us if they believe that personal information has been provided to us unintentionally.

5. Data Security

Because the current version does not intentionally collect or maintain personal information on our servers, there is no user account database or personal-information database maintained by the app developer for the app's core features.

6. Data Retention and Deletion

We do not intentionally collect or retain personal information through the current version of the app. Consequently, there is no personal account or personal-information record in the app that a user needs to request deletion of.

7. Third-Party Services

The current version does not intentionally integrate advertising, analytics, social-network, or user-account services.

The Android operating system and its built-in text-to-speech service may process information according to the policies and settings of the device or service provider. Please refer to the relevant Android/device provider documentation for those services.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

If the app's data practices change, this Privacy Policy will be updated to describe those changes. The effective date at the top of this page will also be updated.
